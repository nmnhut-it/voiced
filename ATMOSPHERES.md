## Summary by Age

- **0-2**: Soft Ghibli style, gentle and nurturing
- **4-6**: Playful children's book illustration, educational wonder
- **8-10**: Epic nature and sci-fi, inspiring exploration
- **13-15**: Futuristic and digital, preparing for technology
- **17-19**: Cinematic and classical, building wisdom
- **21-23**: Spiritual and peaceful, finding balance
